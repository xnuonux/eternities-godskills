# Eternities Chorus local candidate report

## verdict

`eternities-chorus` is certified as a local candidate. It is a portable, agent-neutral capability for truthful social communication and community stewardship. There was no global activation, profile mutation, Pantheon enablement, publication, deployment, push, account mutation, or external write.

## capability shape

Chorus selects the smallest sufficient route among four bounded routes:

1. `identity-and-channel-strategy` resolves identity, audience, channel roles, formats, editorial architecture, profile, capacity, and cadence;
2. `editorial-production` transforms approved evidence into original channel-native stories, posts, captions, short-form scripts, derivatives, and accessible media briefs;
3. `community-operations` plans listening, participation, policy, response triage, moderation readiness, and human escalation without performing public account actions;
4. `measurement-and-stewardship` validates current source-labeled signals, metrics, editorial quality, bounded experiments, findings, and learning decisions.

All routes preserve identity, claim provenance, participant dignity, accessibility, authority, uncertainty, and explicit human ownership. Paid acquisition, prospect enrichment, scraping, scheduling, publishing, outreach, account mutation, fabricated identity, sensitive moderation, crisis communication, and specialist visual or video generation remain refused or handed to a separately authorized specialist.

## proof limits

The receipts prove deterministic contracts, exact provenance, bounded fixtures, artifact hashes, state transitions, and policy gates. They do not prove live-model interpretation of arbitrary language, current platform policy freshness, commercial performance, human moderation quality, or production operation. Public commitments and sensitive human decisions remain human-owned, and any live platform action requires separate current authority and evidence.

## integration boundary

This release is a certified local candidate only. It does not enable a profile, install a global skill, modify an external account, publish content, spend money, or activate a runtime adapter. Local integration preserves those boundaries.
