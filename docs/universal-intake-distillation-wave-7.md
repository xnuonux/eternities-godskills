## Three portable extensions

- **Arcadia:** browser/game runtime lifetime, animation ownership, bounded frame
  timing, resize and input boundaries, stale asset completion, and ownership-aware
  teardown. The reference is engine-neutral and loaded only for relevant work.
- **Chorus:** evidence-bearing stakeholder updates with metric comparability,
  separate recipient/reviewer material, natural audience-appropriate voice, and
  proportionate action coordination. A simple message does not need a ledger.
- **Agora:** account and evidence-room decisions that separate document presence,
  freshness, restrictions, validation and approval. Pipeline, account health,
  metric and escalation signals stay attributable rather than becoming a false
  all-purpose score. Only relevant lanes are used.

No new host, provider, warehouse, private data, fixed filesystem, or paid service
is required by these methods. They neither grant external-action authority nor
require asking again for authority already supplied. They preserve the agent's
judgment and ordinary useful prose rather than forcing a persona or template.
