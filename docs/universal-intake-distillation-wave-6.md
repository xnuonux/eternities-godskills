## New method and source boundary

`cashflow-forecast-scenario-integrity` specializes financial statement
reconciliation. Its contract is settlement-time analysis, not a duplicate general
finance persona. Three source entrypoints were read fully by a non-forked Luna max
worker; the parent reverified every raw hash and repository commit against the
frozen intake. The exact ledger is
[source-dispositions.json](../artifacts/universal-product-v1/wave6/source-dispositions.json).
No acquired scripts, package installs or account actions ran. MIT intake labels
are observations, not a legal-clearance finding.

The final method separates recognition from settlement, tracks partial and
out-of-horizon events, prevents invoice double counting, and distinguishes total
cash from available cash. It treats internal transfers at the declared perimeter,
keeps undrawn credit out of assumed funding, and records reconciliation adjustments
separately. Scenario changes remain attributable to events and dated assumptions.
It does not claim a forecast will occur or imply authority to move money.

The [SEC explanation](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/investorpubsbegfinstmtguide)
was checked for profit/cash and non-cash distinctions; the
[OCC liquidity handbook](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/liquidity/pub-ch-liquidity.pdf)
was checked for time horizons and stress severity/duration. Bank-specific mandates
were not generalized into a universal method. Receiving users need neither source
repositories nor those websites to read the bundled skill.

## Cash-flow consumer exercise and correction

A different fresh Luna max worker applied the first draft to a fictional
three-week forecast with restricted cash, invoices, depreciation, an internal
transfer, an unconfirmed credit line and an out-of-horizon collection delay.
The tables were arithmetically correct. The prose was not: it called the receipt
delta -900 in both W2 and W3, even though W3's period receipt delta is 0 and only
the cumulative balance difference remains -900.

The parent recorded 11 passes and 1 partial against 12 predeclared criteria. That
contradiction is retained in the original candidate; this is not an all-pass
forecast. The final skill now explicitly separates period movement from cumulative
balance deltas and requires agreement between numerical prose and tables. See
[the preserved first result](../artifacts/universal-product-v1/wave6/cashflow-exercise/result.json).

A separate frozen recheck uses a delay that catches up within the displayed
horizon. It exercises the revised wording but changes the task as well; it cannot
prove that the wording caused a performance improvement or replace the first
sample's failure. That new sample passed all 6 criteria: period receipt deltas
`[0,-700,+700]` remained distinct from cumulative balance deltas `[0,-700,0]`.
Both scenarios ended at 600; the reduced second-week liquidity remained visible.
Native host instructions remain present in every worker sample.
