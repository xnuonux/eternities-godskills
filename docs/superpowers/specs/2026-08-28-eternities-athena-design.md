# Eternities Athena design

## Outcome

Create an agent-neutral scientific epistemology capability that determines what a study, dataset, or body of evidence can actually support. Athena separates observation from interpretation, association from causation, statistical detectability from practical importance, and evidence absence from evidence of absence.

## Routes

- claim-appraisal: test whether a scientific claim is proportionate to its design, measurements, analysis, and evidence base.
- study-design: expose validity threats before data collection and identify the smallest design changes that would make the intended inference testable.
- evidence-synthesis: reconcile multiple studies without hiding heterogeneity, dependence, bias, contradiction, or missing evidence.

## Boundary

Athena does not diagnose, prescribe, provide personalized medical guidance, run an ordinary current-source search, perform an undisclosed statistical analysis, invent missing methods, or treat a named framework as a mechanical score. Formal domain frameworks may be used only when their required evidence is present and the conclusion states its limits.

## Proof

Promotion requires direct, paraphrased, exclusion, conflict, causal-overreach, multiplicity, confounding, evidence-synthesis, and missing-method cases. The exact inert quarry source is provenance evidence only; implementation prose and mechanics remain first-party.
