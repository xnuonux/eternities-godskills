# eternities chorus design

date: 2026-08-27

status: approved direction

owner: eternities inc.

## outcome

Create `eternities-chorus`, a portable, agent-neutral godskill for turning a
real identity, audience, purpose, and evidence base into coherent social
strategy, platform-native editorial work, governed community operations, and
measurable learning.

Release seven processes every one of the 106 indexed
`social-media-community` source bodies. Complete-family processing does not
mean adoption. Each source receives exact semantic review and an independently
written disposition. Duplicate, misclassified, unsafe, vendor-bound,
temporally unstable, narrow-template, paid-acquisition, specialist-production,
and external-action material remains visible without becoming Chorus doctrine.

Chorus is not an autonomous publisher, engagement bot, ad-buying system,
impersonator, synthetic-persona factory, analytics provider, visual generator,
video studio, scraper, growth guarantee, or replacement for exact platform,
marketing, research, security, legal, accessibility, or media capabilities.
It owns social and community intent, editorial systems, operational boundaries,
and learning contracts. It delegates account access, specialist production,
current platform facts, and external effects.

## evidence-derived architecture

The product hypothesis is one compact categorical capability with four
dominant routes. Exact review evidence may narrow, split, defer, or reject a
lane, but it cannot silently widen the product boundary.

### route: identity and channel strategy

Reconcile identity, purpose, audience evidence, voice, claims, content pillars,
channel roles, platform fit, editorial boundaries, cadence, capacity, and
success measures. Distinguish source evidence, stakeholder judgment,
observation, inference, and experiment. Do not invent a personality, audience,
testimonial, customer language, or platform rule.

Outputs include a versioned identity and voice contract, audience hypotheses,
channel-role map, content architecture, editorial boundaries, cadence and
capacity model, evidence gaps, and explicit specialist handoffs.

### route: editorial production

Turn approved evidence and strategy into a bounded portfolio of platform-aware
concepts, hooks, drafts, scripts, threads, captions, calls to action, and
repurposing plans. Preserve the core claim while adapting structure, length,
accessibility, and interaction design to each channel. Separate ideation,
drafting, factual review, voice review, asset production, and publication.

Outputs include an idea portfolio, editorial brief, platform variants,
provenance and claim notes, accessibility annotations, asset briefs, review
states, and a publication-ready package that remains unposted.

### route: community operations

Design bounded listening, response, moderation, relationship, calendar,
collaboration, escalation, and crisis workflows. Respect participant dignity,
consent, privacy, community rules, account authority, rate limits, and
anti-spam boundaries. Human review owns sensitive replies, conflict,
moderation sanctions, public commitments, and crisis communication.

Outputs include a community charter, response and moderation matrix, escalation
paths, engagement queue, calendar, collaboration contract, incident log, and
an explicit ledger of proposed or performed external effects.

### route: measurement and stewardship

Define observations and experiments that connect content and community work to
declared goals without confusing reach with value. Reconcile platform metrics,
qualitative feedback, cohorts, attribution limits, content costs, brand and
community health, safety signals, and learning decisions. Treat platform data
as partial and temporally bounded.

Outputs include a measurement contract, metric dictionary, experiment ledger,
content and community scorecard, anomaly and reputation watch, findings with
confidence labels, decisions, and a next-cycle learning plan.

## shared laws

1. Identity, audience, purpose, evidence, and authority precede output.
2. One dominant route owns each operation; cross-route missions use explicit
   artifacts and never recursively invoke Chorus.
3. Drafting, saving, scheduling, publishing, replying, moderating, scraping,
   messaging, buying media, and changing an account are distinct effects.
4. No external effect occurs without exact target binding, matching authority,
   preview when meaningful, and a receipt.
5. Never fabricate lived experience, identity, audience evidence, endorsement,
   customer language, relationship, statistic, event, scarcity, or consensus.
6. Voice adaptation preserves truth and identity; it does not imitate a living
   person closely enough to mislead or erase provenance.
7. Platform-native does not mean platform-dependent. Temporal platform facts,
   limits, policies, formats, APIs, and algorithms require current authoritative
   evidence when consequential.
8. Accessibility, privacy, consent, safety, and participant dignity are design
   inputs, not final review veneers.
9. Engagement is not the objective by default. Every metric maps to a declared
   goal, known limitations, and a decision it can actually inform.
10. Experiments declare hypotheses, costs, stop conditions, confounders, and
    learning criteria before results are interpreted.
11. Paid acquisition, lead enrichment, security reconnaissance, financial
    sentiment, and specialist asset production remain separate capabilities.
12. The smallest sufficient route and specialist set wins. Chorus does not
    absorb generic marketing strategy, visual production, platform operation,
    or external execution.

## review lanes and dispositions

The queue names suggest four review lanes, but final clusters come only from
exact body evidence:

- strategy and identity: audience, niche, voice, profile, content pillars,
  channel strategy, calendars, and campaign framing;
- editorial systems: ideation, hooks, posts, threads, scripts, repurposing,
  formatting, carousels, short-form video, and asset briefs;
- community operations: listening, engagement, moderation, outreach,
  scheduling, collaboration, public relations, and crisis response;
- measurement and stewardship: analytics, testing, dashboards, social
  listening, reputation, attribution, and learning loops.

Likely false positives include infrastructure, generic security, financial
trading sentiment, lead enrichment, and unrelated runtime administration.
Paid ads and account-based acquisition are reviewed but normally deferred to
the marketing family. Image, graphic, infographic, thumbnail, animation, and
video production are reviewed as specialist handoff contracts unless they add
distinct social direction behavior. Scrapers, schedulers, platform APIs, and
account mutation are external-effect boundaries rather than implicit tools.

## routing and composition

Chorus routes from ordinary outcomes with family `social-media-community`.
Its compact routing card exposes only the capabilities supported by promoted
clusters. It declares local read and local write effects. Network research,
scraping, messaging, scheduling, publishing, replying, moderation actions,
account mutation, ad spend, asset purchase, and commercial commitments require
separately matched capabilities and authority.

Logical compatibility may include Oracle for current evidence, Aegis for
authority and risk, Muse for visual or narrative art direction, Forge for
repository implementation, Agora for client-service context, the future
marketing-family capability for positioning and paid growth, Hermes for
authorized integrations, and exact document or media capabilities.
Compatibility never authorizes automatic composition or self-delegation.

## evaluation

All critical cases cover direct, paraphrased, contextual, exclusion, conflict,
authority, effect, temporal-evidence, factuality, identity, safety, privacy,
accessibility, and human-verdict behavior. Cases must separate all supported
routes, reject routine copy edits, defer specialist production and paid media,
refuse unauthorized external actions, and preserve platform, legal, privacy,
licensing, identity, impersonation, moderation, and commercial boundaries.

Promotion requires exact 106-source review and cluster reconciliation, no
copied prose, no source instruction execution, all critical cases passing,
every policy-kind threshold, resolved effects, entrypoint size at or below
4,000 estimated tokens, no critical regression, deterministic receipts, and at
least one measured improvement over the baseline.

Static fixtures do not prove live-model interpretation, factual truth,
platform reach, community health, content quality, account safety, policy
compliance, accessibility compliance, current platform behavior, commercial
performance, or production operation.

## corpus and router state

All 106 family rows advance to `card-reviewed` only after exact review. All
reviewed rows advance to `clustered` only after exact membership reconciliation.
Only source rows included in a promoted candidate advance through synthesized,
evaluated, and promoted evidence. Exact counts are derived from certified
cluster decisions and must not be guessed in advance.

Router v3 remains an immutable nine-card historical checkpoint. Release seven
creates a separately receipted router v4 only after Chorus passes promotion.
Historical receipts and checkpoints continue validating the state they
certified. No global runtime adapter is enabled by this release.

## acceptance criteria

1. All 106 queue source ids have one exact review whose digest matches the
   inspected body.
2. All 106 reviewed sources appear exactly once in deterministic cluster
   evidence with an explicit candidate, deferred, or rejected decision.
3. Every promoted behavior traces to exact review and cluster evidence; no
   deferred or rejected source contributes to the core contract.
4. Chorus exposes the smallest evidence-supported route set and remains under
   the 4,000 estimated-token ceiling.
5. Unnamed natural-language outcomes route without slash commands, product
   names, or aliases; removing all aliases preserves exact routing results.
6. External effects, account authority, human-sensitive decisions, temporal
   platform facts, and specialist handoffs fail closed when unresolved.
7. Promotion passes every all-critical case and the repository promotion
   policy with no critical regression.
8. Corpus totals reconcile exactly from immutable source, review, cluster,
   synthesis, evaluation, and promotion evidence across two builds.
9. Router v3 remains byte-verifiable and router v4 is deterministic across two
   builds.
10. The complete repository suite passes with zero failed and zero skipped
    tests after local integration.
11. The report distinguishes deterministic proof from unverified live-model,
    platform, community, legal, accessibility, commercial, and production
    claims.
12. No push, publication, deployment, profile mutation, global activation,
    Pantheon enablement, provider mutation, account action, or external
    communication occurs.
