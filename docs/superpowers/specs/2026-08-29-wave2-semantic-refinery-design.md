# Wave 2 Semantic Refinery Design

date: 2026-08-29

status: approved by founder direction

owner: Eternities Inc.

## outcome

Advance every one of the 3,448 accepted canonical Wave 2 facets from structural
atlas evidence into exact semantic review, behavioral clustering, overlap
adjudication, and a terminal synthesis disposition. Promote only independently
written capabilities that preserve or improve the strongest verified behavior
of both the original 4,741-source refinery and the Wave 2 quarry.

## correction to the prior completion boundary

The existing total-infusion certificate proves deterministic accounting for
7,776 source bodies, exact duplicate folding, static security disposition,
structural facet extraction, and bounded retrieval. It does not prove semantic
correctness or deep refinery completion. This phase preserves that certificate
as a structural checkpoint and adds a distinct semantic certificate. It must
never rewrite structural completion into a semantic claim retroactively.

## exact source boundary

- 7,776 Wave 2 source bodies remain in the terminal structural ledger.
- 4,195 exact duplicate aliases inherit their canonical body's semantic result.
- 3,581 canonical bodies are the only semantic-review identities.
- 133 security-rejected canonical bodies retain rejection and are never read or
  promoted by this phase.
- 3,448 accepted canonical facets require one exact semantic review each.
- the original 4,741-source semantic corpus remains independent comparison
  evidence and may not be silently replaced or counted as Wave 2 review.
- the Lunari first-party quarry remains separately receipted first-party
  evidence and may support overlap decisions without changing these counts.

## review contract

Each accepted canonical facet receives one independently authored review bound
to its facet id, canonical source id, body digest, security evidence, family,
and exact source body. Reviews contain neutral intent examples, inputs,
operations, outputs, effects, failure behavior, exclusions, useful invariants,
material risks, provider and project coupling, and one disposition:

- `independent-implementation`
- `pattern-reference`
- `deferred`
- `rejected`

No review may claim promotion, copy source prose, execute source instructions,
or infer license clearance. Reviews are stored in deterministic family packets
of at most 25 canonical facets. Every packet records its reviewer identity and
method. Validation fails closed on stale digests, duplicate coverage, missing
fields, unsupported effects, copied descriptions, or unreviewed facets.

## clustering and overlap

Reviewed facets are clustered by behavioral contract rather than name,
repository, popularity, or embedding proximity. Every review belongs to exactly
one Wave 2 cluster. Each cluster records an intent, relationship, rationale,
member review digests, and one synthesis decision: `candidate`, `deferred`, or
`rejected`.

Candidate clusters are compared against:

1. all promoted Godskill and refinery contracts;
2. all original 4,741-source semantic clusters and promoted syntheses;
3. the ten Lunari first-party contracts;
4. other Wave 2 clusters with overlapping inputs, operations, outputs, and
   failure boundaries.

The overlap result is one of `covered-stronger`, `extend-existing`,
`new-operational-skill`, `new-godskill`, `ultragodskill-candidate`, `deferred`,
or `rejected`. `covered-stronger` requires an exact mechanism comparison, not a
shared label.

## synthesis and promotion

The refinery produces the smallest independently written capability hierarchy
earned by evidence. It does not target a quota. A candidate may become a
sovereign atom, an operational skill, an extension to an existing Godskill, a
new categorical Godskill, or a cross-category Ultragodskill.

Every synthesized capability must have a neutral contract, discriminating
entrypoint, routing card, direct/paraphrased/contextual and negative fixtures,
effect and authority boundaries, provenance dispositions, executable or
structural baselines where possible, token measurement, and a promotion
receipt. Promotion requires every critical case, no critical regression,
resolved effects, bounded context cost, and at least one measured improvement
over the strongest applicable baseline. Incomplete evidence remains cold.

## automatic capability-gap routing

Ordinary requests should route to the smallest promoted capability. When no
promoted card completely covers a consequential request, the router may invoke
a bounded capability-gap lookup automatically. That lookup reads at most five
compact reviewed cluster cards, never source bodies, and cannot expand effects
or authority. It either selects an already promoted capability, hands a pattern
to the sovereign refinery as cold design evidence, or records an unresolved
catalog gap. Explicit quarry wording is not required once semantic
certification exists.

## completion contract

The phase is complete only when all of the following are true:

- exactly 3,448 accepted canonical facets have valid semantic reviews;
- exactly 3,448 reviews have one cluster membership;
- every cluster has a terminal synthesis decision;
- every candidate cluster has an exact overlap disposition;
- every synthesized artifact is evaluated and terminally promoted, deferred,
  or rejected;
- exact duplicates inherit one canonical result without duplicate prompt cost;
- all generated artifacts rebuild byte-identically from exact inputs;
- automatic gap lookup is bounded, authority-preserving, and fixture-certified;
- the full repository test suite passes from the merged checkout;
- an independent adversarial review has no unresolved critical or important
  findings;
- no third-party code or instruction is executed and no global profile,
  publication, deployment, push, spending, or external account is changed.

## proof limits

The final certificate may prove local evidence coverage, exact source and review
digests, deterministic clustering, fixture behavior, and measured candidate
results. It cannot prove universal semantic truth, legal clearance, production
behavior, arbitrary live-model interpretation, reviewer independence beyond
the recorded process, or safety of executing third-party code.
