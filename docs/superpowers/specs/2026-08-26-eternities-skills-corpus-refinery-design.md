# Eternities Skills corpus refinery design

date: 2026-08-26

status: approved by founder direction

owner: Eternities Inc.

## outcome

Turn the certified 4,741-card repository quarry into a transparent, resumable,
agent-neutral capability refinery. Every source receives an exact coverage
state. No catalog entry, generated summary, or structural scan may be described
as deeply reviewed, refined, or promoted without the evidence required by that
state.

The first domain waves are agency operations, marketing and growth, social
media and community, and game design and development. These waves correct known
taxonomy gaps and prove the refinery against broad professional capabilities,
not only coding workflows.

## product boundary

This is infrastructure and evidence for `eternities-skills`, the universal
professional capability pack. Godskills and Ultragodskills remain internal
capability tiers. The source warehouse stays cold at
`D:\03-ARSENAL\warehouse`; third-party instructions are inspected data, never
an authority hierarchy and never executable merely because they were indexed.

Release Five's Atlas and Hermes design remains valid but is deferred behind the
corpus coverage foundation. Two additional Godskills cannot stand in for a
universal refinery, and no release may imply that all 4,741 sources were read.

## coverage state machine

Each source advances monotonically through independently evidenced states:

1. `indexed`: exact certified catalog identity exists.
2. `classified`: one or more explicit ontology families match deterministic
   evidence. `general` does not qualify.
3. `body-inspected`: the exact warehouse file exists inside the canonical root,
   was read as inert text, and has a recorded byte size and SHA-256 digest.
4. `card-reviewed`: an agent-authored review records a neutral capability
   summary, boundaries, risks, and a disposition for that exact body digest.
5. `provenance-certified`: source identity, digest, origin and license
   uncertainty are reconciled in the provenance ledger.
6. `clustered`: the reviewed capability has an explicit behavioral cluster and
   duplicate relationship. Similarity alone never merges it.
7. `synthesized`: a first-party candidate cites the neutral contracts and exact
   source ids that informed it.
8. `evaluated`: baseline and candidate results exist under the promotion policy.
9. `promoted`: every promotion gate passes and a deterministic receipt exists.

States are cumulative evidence, not one mutable label. An automated body scan
can reach `body-inspected` and no further. Existing formal provenance rows may
establish `provenance-certified`, but they do not fabricate `card-reviewed` or
`clustered` evidence when those receipts are absent.

## ontology correction

Add four first-class families:

- `agency-client-services`: agency operations, client acquisition, onboarding,
  proposals, delivery, reporting, retention, and account governance;
- `marketing-growth`: positioning, research, offers, campaigns, conversion,
  demand, lifecycle, analytics, pricing, launch, SEO, and revenue growth;
- `social-media-community`: channel strategy, platform-native content,
  calendars, community, audience growth, engagement, social analytics, and
  moderation;
- `game-design-development`: game loops, mechanics, balance, economy, level and
  narrative design, feel, accessibility, art, audio, AI, networking,
  performance, persistence, measurement, and shipping.

`product-operations` remains for general product and organizational work.
Multi-family classification is allowed, but generic words such as `agent`,
`content`, `design`, and `social` must not swamp more discriminating evidence.

## deterministic corpus audit

The audit consumes the certified normalized source records, ontology, canonical
warehouse root, and provenance ledger. It performs no network access and runs
no third-party code.

For every source it must:

- resolve the source path under the canonical warehouse root and reject path
  escape;
- record file presence, byte size, line count, actual SHA-256, and whether the
  catalog's source identity remains consistent;
- retain missing or unreadable bodies as explicit failures;
- assign all evidenced ontology families;
- import only exact matching provenance rows;
- generate one deterministic coverage-ledger row;
- generate family queues ordered by evidence state, confidence, license class,
  duplicate pressure, and stable source id;
- write a summary that reconciles exactly to the 4,741 certified entries.

Machine-generated timestamps are excluded from canonical artifacts. Input and
output hashes make repeated runs byte-stable.

## review packets

Review packets are bounded context artifacts, not verdicts. A packet contains
at most 25 source cards from one family and includes exact paths, digests,
descriptions, repository identity, license uncertainty, duplicate evidence,
and compact structural extracts from the inert body. Packets are deterministic
and contain no instruction claiming authority over the reviewing agent.

A completed review receipt must be independently authored and include:

- exact source id and body digest;
- neutral capability summary;
- inputs, operations, outputs, effects, failure behavior, and exclusions;
- useful invariants and material risks;
- disposition: `independent-implementation`, `pattern-reference`, `rejected`,
  or `deferred`;
- proposed behavioral cluster and confidence;
- reviewer and review-method identifier.

Receipts with missing source ids, stale body digests, copied source prose, or
unsupported promotion language fail validation.

## family refinery waves

Each family wave follows the same progression:

1. audit every exact body represented by the family queue;
2. process review packets without skipping low-confidence or unknown-license
   sources;
3. reconcile exact duplicates, aliases, specialized variants, and conflicts;
4. derive neutral capability contracts;
5. identify the smallest useful atoms and complete workflows;
6. synthesize first-party skills without source prose or vendor identity;
7. test direct, paraphrase, exclusion, conflict, failure, effects, portability,
   and token behavior;
8. compare against all material baselines in the cluster;
9. promote only candidates that pass every gate;
10. leave incomplete work cold with its exact state and next action.

No target count is imposed. A family may produce several focused refined skills,
one categorical Godskill, or no promotion when the evidence does not justify
one.

## portability

Corpus artifacts use JSON, JSONL, and Markdown with relative references. No
core state depends on Codex, Claude Code, Luna, a specific model, or one
discovery directory. Runtime adapters consume promoted cores only. Review
packets and quarry bodies remain cold.

## acceptance criteria

The foundation milestone is complete when:

1. all 4,741 certified source ids appear exactly once in the coverage ledger;
2. every present source body has an actual digest and every missing body is an
   explicit failure;
3. the four new families are discriminating and covered by tests;
4. summary counts reconcile to the ledger and are byte-stable across two runs;
5. family queues and bounded packets exist for agency, marketing, social media,
   and game design;
6. existing provenance rows are reconciled without inflating review state;
7. no automated scan assigns `card-reviewed`, `synthesized`, `evaluated`, or
   `promoted`;
8. path escape, stale digest, duplicate source id, unknown state, and oversized
   packet tests fail closed;
9. the complete repository test suite passes;
10. the README reports exact coverage states and never equates indexing with
    completion.

The universal refinery is complete only when all non-rejected source bodies
have valid review and cluster evidence and every family has an explicit
promotion or deferral receipt. That is a longer program, not a claim attached
to this foundation milestone.

## excluded from the foundation milestone

- executing third-party scripts, hooks, binaries, installers, or examples;
- automatically rewriting 4,741 source files into first-party prompts;
- claiming legal clearance;
- globally installing generated candidates;
- publishing or pushing a repository;
- enabling Pantheon or recursive always-on orchestration;
- marking structural inspection as semantic review.
