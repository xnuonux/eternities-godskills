# Eternities Godskills Full Corpus Completion Design

## purpose

Complete the universal refinery honestly across all 4,741 certified source bodies. Completion means every source has one exact semantic review owner, every non-rejected source has exact cluster evidence, every owner family has an explicit promotion or deferral receipt, every promoted capability is independently written and evaluated, and the portable router selects promoted capabilities without aliases or full-corpus prompt loading.

Completion does not require promoting every source or creating a fixed number of skills. Rejected, deferred, duplicate, unsafe, temporal, provider-bound, demo-only, or category-false-positive material remains visible with exact evidence. No source prose or vendor identity becomes first-party doctrine merely because it was indexed.

## canonical ownership

The ontology is multi-label evidence and therefore cannot itself be a review ledger. One source may match several families, but semantic review and cluster membership must remain unique.

Ownership is deterministic:

1. an existing validated review row preserves its canonical `familyId`;
2. an unreviewed source is owned by the first family in its classifier-ordered `families` array;
3. a source with no classified family is owned by `general`;
4. all additional family ids remain `secondaryFamilies` and may inform cross-family handoffs, but cannot create another review or cluster row;
5. every source id appears exactly once in the ownership manifest and all owner queues together.

## review waves

Every owner queue is split into stable packets of at most 25 sources. Each source body is read only as inert evidence. A review records exact source and body digests, independently worded inputs, operations, outputs, effects, failure behavior, exclusions, useful invariants, risks, two commandless intent examples, disposition, proposed cluster, confidence, copied-prose false, promotion-claim false, and legacy aliases only as non-authoritative evidence.

Parallel workers may author disjoint packets. A merge gate rejects duplicate ids, stale digests, source-id scaffolding, normalized duplicate intent text, copied prose, unsupported promotion language, path escape, or missing contract fields. No review is accepted solely from filenames, headings, command names, popularity, or licenses.

## cluster and capability decisions

After one owner family is completely reviewed, its sources are reconciled into exact behavior clusters. Every reviewed source appears exactly once in that owner family cluster set. Clusters receive candidate, deferred, or rejected decisions. Candidate clusters must preserve a coherent neutral workflow and exact review digests. Deferred and rejected clusters remain cold and contribute no promoted source coverage.

One family may extend an existing Godskill, create one or more new focused skills, create a categorical Godskill, or promote nothing. Evidence decides. Names are assigned only after cluster boundaries exist. New capabilities must be agent-neutral, commandless, independently written, bounded by explicit authority and effects, non-recursive, compact, and portable across coding agents and general agents.

## evaluation and routing

Every candidate receives direct, paraphrased, exclusion, conflict, failure, effect, portability, and token cases. Promotion requires all critical cases, policy thresholds, exact candidate-source coverage, resolved effects, token compliance, no critical regression, and at least one permitted fixture-baseline improvement. Fixture evidence is never represented as live-model or production-performance proof.

Before changing the live router, freeze the preceding router bytes. Each promoted capability adds one compact routing card with unnamed outcomes, empty aliases, exact effects, authority, risk, preconditions, and context cost. Router certification requires alias-removal equality, smallest-sufficient selection, bounded retrieval across 5,000 cards, selected-entrypoint-only disclosure, and fail-closed unresolved decisions.

## staged releases

Owner families are completed smallest-first unless dependency evidence requires another order. The default sequence is:

1. release-publishing, data-infrastructure, automation-mcp-integrations, product-operations, writing-narrative-canon;
2. audio-voice-media, implementation-engineering, governance-security, architecture-specification, web-interface-accessibility;
3. debugging-recovery, visual-3d-motion, repository-source-research, knowledge-memory-context;
4. verification-evidence, general;
5. agent-orchestration;
6. universal reconciliation, router certification, documentation, independent review, and local integration.

Families within a stage may be reviewed in parallel because their ownership sets are disjoint. Clustering, synthesis, promotion, live corpus rebuilds, and router advancement are serialized at family integration boundaries.

## completion gates

The repository is fully complete only when:

1. all 4,741 source ids have one validated review row and one owner;
2. every non-rejected source has exact cluster evidence and every rejected source has an explicit reviewed rejection disposition;
3. owner queues, review rows, clusters, syntheses, evaluations, receipts, and coverage ledgers reconcile without duplicate source advancement;
4. every owner family has an explicit certified promotion or deferral receipt;
5. every promoted capability routes from unnamed outcomes after all aliases are removed;
6. all historical router and release evidence remains frozen and hash-exact;
7. two routing builds and two coverage builds are byte-identical;
8. the complete test suite, promotion verification, receipt reconciliation, and `git diff --check` pass on the integrated local main branch;
9. an independent reviewer reports no unresolved critical or important issue;
10. no external activation, profile mutation, publication, deployment, push, spending, account mutation, outreach, or Pantheon enablement occurred.

## proof limits

The refinery proves local deterministic artifacts, declared contracts, exact source lineage, evaluation fixtures, routing policy, and state transitions. It does not prove legal clearance, arbitrary live-model interpretation, production reliability, current provider behavior, current law or policy, commercial performance, or the quality of future external decisions.
