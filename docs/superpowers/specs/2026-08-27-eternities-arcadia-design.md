## evidence-derived architecture

The approved product hypothesis is one compact categorical capability with four dominant routes. The hypothesis is not allowed to override review evidence. If exact review proves a route incoherent, high-risk, duplicate, or better owned by an existing capability, the cluster is narrowed, delegated, deferred, or rejected before synthesis.

### route: game direction

Turn a game intent into a bounded, testable design contract. Reconcile player promise, target audience, platform constraints, core verbs, mechanics, loops, progression, pacing, difficulty, narrative, randomness, and ethical economy. Separate design intent, heuristics, telemetry, playtest observation, and proposal. Do not treat genre conventions or numeric ranges as universal laws.

Outputs include a player-promise contract, minimum fun proof, mechanic and loop model, progression and pacing curves, economy assumptions, narrative constraints, design risks, playtest questions, and explicit deferred production work.

### route: runtime systems

Settle game-specific system boundaries before implementation. Cover engine and dimensionality decisions, simulation and render loops, input, saves and migration, multiplayer topology, moment-to-moment AI, deterministic state, performance budgets, and platform constraints. Prefer the simplest mechanism that satisfies the observed requirement. Engine APIs and package installation remain delegated and current-evidence dependent.

Outputs include a runtime contract, state boundaries, timing model, input map, persistence schema and migration policy, networking authority model, AI decision model, performance budgets, failure modes, and implementation handoff.

### route: player experience

Direct the sensory and accessibility system that makes mechanics legible and satisfying. Cover game feel, animation, art direction, asset provenance, generated-art disclosure, audio, voice, VFX, feedback hierarchy, remappable input, captions, contrast, reduced motion, difficulty support, and device-tier fallbacks. Presentation cannot conceal an unproven loop or increase evidence confidence.

Outputs include a feedback contract, visual and audio hierarchy, animation and VFX budgets, asset and license inventory, accessibility contract, fallback tiers, acceptance captures, and specialist handoffs.

### route: proof and release

Prove declared game behavior through executable probes, reachability checks, frame-time and variance evidence, first-session traces, real-device evidence, shortfall lists, and human judgment. Prepare release readiness without publishing. Platform, store, privacy, rating, signing, legal, and policy claims require current official evidence and matching authority.

Outputs include a measurement contract, deterministic probes, device and performance matrix, reachability audit, first-session trace, regression evidence, unresolved shortfalls, human verdict packet, and release-readiness handoff.

## shared laws

1. A playable, measurable loop precedes decorative breadth and release claims.
2. One dominant route owns each operation. Cross-route missions use explicit artifacts, not recursive Arcadia invocation.
3. Declared behavior is not driven behavior. Code presence is not reachability, playability, feel, or fun.
4. Every design number is labeled as observed, measured, inferred, assumed, heuristic, or proposed.
5. Human play evidence remains authoritative for fun, clarity, comfort, fairness, and final acceptance.
6. Deterministic probes verify narrow claims; they do not replace representative play or real-device evidence.
7. Platform, engine, package, store, accessibility-law, privacy, and policy facts are temporally unstable and require current authoritative evidence when consequential.
8. Performance begins with explicit frame, memory, thermal, draw, allocation, network, and device-tier budgets.
9. Save data, networking, economy, generated assets, voice, and external publication preserve authority, provenance, privacy, migration, and reversibility boundaries.
10. Monetization cannot use dark patterns, fabricated scarcity, deceptive odds, coercive friction, or unverified commercial claims.
11. Accessibility is a design input and acceptance dimension, not a final compliance veneer.
12. The smallest sufficient route and specialist set wins. Arcadia does not absorb generic implementation, visual production, or publishing work.
