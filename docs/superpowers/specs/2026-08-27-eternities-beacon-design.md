# eternities beacon design

date: 2026-08-27

status: approved autonomous direction

owner: eternities inc.

## outcome

Create `eternities-beacon`, a portable, agent-neutral godskill for turning
verifiable market evidence into positioning, offers, go-to-market systems,
conversion and lifecycle improvements, and measured growth decisions.

Release eight processes every one of the 291 indexed `marketing-growth`
source bodies. Complete-family processing does not mean adoption. Every source
receives an exact semantic review and independently written disposition.
Duplicates, false positives, provider-bound procedures, unsafe acquisition,
temporally unstable tactics, narrow templates, external account operations,
and unsupported performance claims remain visible without becoming Beacon
doctrine.

Beacon is not an ad account operator, autonomous campaign launcher, sales bot,
scraper, contact-enrichment system, deceptive persuasion engine, guarantee of
revenue, legal or privacy authority, analytics provider, social publisher,
visual studio, or replacement for current platform research. It owns the
reasoning and artifact contracts that connect market truth to bounded growth
decisions. Exact tools and separately authorized capabilities own external
effects.

## evidence-derived architecture hypothesis

Exact reviews may narrow or split these routes. Promotion may include only
routes supported by candidate clusters.

### market truth and positioning

Reconcile customer, category, competitor, product, problem, context, and proof
into a source-labeled market model. Separate observation, supplied fact,
inference, hypothesis, and experiment. Define audience segments, jobs and
constraints, differentiation, category frame, claims, reasons to believe,
message hierarchy, evidence gaps, and falsification conditions.

### offer and commercial architecture

Turn verified value and economics into an offer system: outcome, scope,
mechanism, packaging, pricing hypothesis, risk reversal, qualification,
objections, proof, boundaries, and unit-economics assumptions. Refuse false
scarcity, fabricated social proof, hidden conditions, dark patterns, and prices
presented as optimized without evidence.

### go-to-market and demand systems

Design launch, campaign, channel, creative-testing, content, partner, organic,
earned, and paid-demand plans from a declared goal, audience, budget boundary,
capacity, and authority. Produce briefs, experiment portfolios, sequencing,
handoffs, stop conditions, and effect ledgers. Planning never implies account
access, media buying, publication, outreach, or commercial commitment.

### conversion and lifecycle systems

Map journeys from first contact through activation, conversion, retention,
expansion, referral, and recovery. Diagnose friction through evidence, define
testable page, funnel, onboarding, email, pricing, and retention changes, and
preserve consent, accessibility, truthful choice, and reversible experiments.

### growth measurement and stewardship

Define metric meanings, causal assumptions, attribution limits, cohorts,
guardrails, costs, confidence, experiment design, anomaly handling, and
decision rules. Distinguish activity from outcomes and local uplift from total
business value. Produce findings, uncertainty, stop or scale decisions, and a
next-cycle learning plan.

## shared laws

1. Market, customer, product, economic, and authority evidence precede claims.
2. Facts, supplied assertions, observations, inferences, hypotheses, forecasts,
   and experiments remain visibly distinct.
3. No customer language, testimonial, benchmark, case study, scarcity,
   endorsement, competitor claim, or performance result is fabricated.
4. Persuasion preserves informed choice, participant dignity, and truthful
   representation. Dark patterns and coercive optimization are rejected.
5. One dominant route owns each operation. Cross-route missions exchange
   explicit artifacts and never recursively invoke Beacon.
6. Research, drafting, saving, publishing, outreach, account mutation, media
   buying, pricing changes, and commercial commitments are distinct effects.
7. External effects require exact target binding, matching authority, preview
   when meaningful, budget and stop bounds, and a receipt.
8. Current platform rules, market facts, competitor states, prices, laws, and
   benchmarks require current authoritative evidence when consequential.
9. Metrics have definitions, owners, windows, denominators, limitations, and a
   decision they can actually inform.
10. Experiments declare hypothesis, population, intervention, guardrails,
    sample and duration logic, confounders, stop conditions, and learning rules
    before results are interpreted.
11. Correlation, attribution, and causation are not interchangeable.
12. Privacy, consent, accessibility, safety, deliverability, brand trust, and
    long-term value are optimization constraints, not cleanup tasks.
13. Paid acquisition, personal-data enrichment, account operation, financial
    execution, specialist media production, and legal judgment retain separate
    capability and authority boundaries.
14. The smallest sufficient route and specialist set wins. Beacon does not
    absorb social community operations, generic copy editing, visual
    production, provider APIs, or sales execution.

## routing and composition

Beacon routes from ordinary outcomes with family `marketing-growth`. Its
compact routing card exposes only promoted cluster behavior. Core effects are
local read and local write. Network research, scraping, contact enrichment,
messaging, publication, account mutation, ad spend, purchases, pricing changes,
and commercial commitments remain unresolved until separately authorized.

Logical compatibility may include Oracle for current market evidence, Aegis
for authority and risk, Chorus for social and community systems, Agora for
client-service context, Muse for visual and narrative direction, Atlas for data
contracts, Hermes for authorized integrations, and exact platform or document
skills. Compatibility never authorizes automatic composition.

## evaluation and promotion

All critical cases cover direct, paraphrased, contextual, exclusion, conflict,
authority, effect, temporal-evidence, factuality, privacy, accessibility,
commercial, measurement, and human-verdict behavior. Cases must separate every
supported route, reject routine copy edits, and fail closed for fabricated
proof, coercive patterns, unauthorized outreach, account mutation, spending,
pricing changes, and unsupported growth guarantees.

Promotion requires exact 291-source review and cluster reconciliation, no
copied prose, no source execution, all critical cases passing, every policy
threshold, resolved core effects, an entrypoint at or below 4,000 estimated
tokens, no critical regression, deterministic receipts, and at least one
measured improvement over baseline.

Static fixtures do not prove live-model interpretation, factual truth, market
fit, causal uplift, legal compliance, platform operation, deliverability,
revenue, or production performance.
