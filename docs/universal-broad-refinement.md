## Portability and metadata decisions

- Each product skill has SKILL.md, skill.json, and a relative references/methods.md resource. No product resource points to a historical file, absent companion, personal path, or external service.
- Each skill.json uses only the planned top-level fields: id, category, summary, triggers, antiTriggers, taskTypes, related, resources, maturity, and provenance. taskTypes is normalized to the finite vocabulary research, plan, build, verify, recover, orchestrate, create, and communicate.
- related contains only stable broad IDs present in the product pack. Relationships are advisory routing context and do not grant authority.
- Anti-triggers describe a genuinely mismatched task or an effect missing its required target, owner, authority, or evidence; they do not reject normal research, drafting, implementation, or publication preparation. Logos includes article, canon-preserving narrative, storytelling, and source-truth language for natural task lookup.
- Omnibus resolves a host-supplied pack location or explicit product root first. Two directory levels above the skill is only an intact-pack convenience. Standalone skill folders and runtime pack copies may have different parents; missing catalogs fall back to visible metadata/manual indexing or an explicit unresolved state. No catalog is written.
