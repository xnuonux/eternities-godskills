# Eternities Beacon local candidate report

## verdict

`eternities-beacon` is certified as a local candidate. it is a portable, agent-neutral capability for evidence-bound marketing and growth work. there was no global activation, profile mutation, Pantheon enablement, publication, deployment, push, account mutation, spending, commercial commitment, external write, or external market action.

## capability shape

Beacon selects the smallest sufficient route among six bounded routes:

1. `market-truth-and-positioning` reconciles dated customer, category, competitor, product, and proof evidence into falsifiable positioning and claim lineage;
2. `offer-and-commercial-architecture` develops outcome, scope, packaging, pricing hypotheses, qualification, proof, risk, and unit-economics assumptions without accepting a commercial commitment;
3. `discoverability-and-search-systems` diagnoses crawl, rendering, indexing, information architecture, structured data, content trust, entities, local visibility, and answer eligibility;
4. `go-to-market-and-demand-systems` designs launch sequences, channel and partnership portfolios, capacity boundaries, demand experiments, and explicit effect ledgers;
5. `conversion-and-lifecycle-systems` diagnoses acquisition, activation, conversion, onboarding, retention, expansion, referral, and recovery while preserving consent and accessible choice;
6. `growth-measurement-and-stewardship` defines metrics, instrumentation, cohorts, attribution limits, forecasts, experiments, guardrails, and scale or stop decisions.

All routes require truthful claims, source dates, uncertainty, customer-data purpose, consent, accessibility, metric definitions, effect authority, and explicit human ownership. scraping, enrichment, outreach, account mutation, publication, media buying, spending, pricing mutation, commercial acceptance, unsupported current facts, fabricated proof, coercion, and specialist execution remain refused or handed to an authorized specialist.

## proof limits

The receipts prove deterministic contracts, exact provenance, bounded fixtures, artifact hashes, state transitions, and local policy gates. They do not prove live-model interpretation of arbitrary language, platform policy freshness, commercial performance, production operation, or the quality of future human decisions. Current external facts require fresh evidence. Spending, commercial acceptance, public claims, customer-data use, and live platform actions remain human-owned and separately authorized.

## integration boundary

This release is a certified local candidate only. It does not install a global skill, enable a runtime adapter, alter a profile, mutate an account, publish, contact anyone, spend money, set a live price, accept a contract, or perform any external market action. Local integration preserves those boundaries.
