# Forge Telos Closure Specification

## outcome

Add an opt-in, agent-neutral closure route to Eternities Forge for requests that explicitly demand an ideal-bar, perfection, or structural-closure review of consequential completed work.

## contract

The route must:

- begin from an explicit claim, invariant, acceptance evidence, and inspected artifact or diff;
- distinguish a missing defect from a structurally prevented defect;
- track one defect shape across relocated manifestations and downstream consumers;
- use fresh adversarial review when available without treating reviewers as evidence by themselves;
- require a changed or newly exercised proof surface for each repair;
- preserve authority, scope, rollback, and external-mutation boundaries;
- record per-round findings, changes, evidence, regressions, and measurable gain;
- stop only as `closed`, `saturated`, `blocked`, or `budget-exhausted`;
- stop immediately on authority loss, critical regression, destructive uncertainty, or an invalid oracle;
- enforce declared iteration, time, token, and mutation budgets, with saturation after two rounds without new material evidence or measurable gain.

The route must not promise metaphysical perfection, loop indefinitely, use completion tokens as proof, invoke ruflo, copy source prose, or become the default for ordinary delivery and review.
