# eternities-muse reviewer v1

artifact-required: true
self-certification-allowed: false

## success condition

One dominant route produces a source-grounded visual contract whose identity, accessibility, performance, uncertainty, delegation, and rejection criteria are explicit enough for implementation or verification.

## required outputs

- confidence-labeled visual evidence or art-direction contract
- token, component, narrative, motion, media, or acceptance system
- bounded specialist handoff
- explicit unknowns, budgets, invariants, and rejection criteria

## rejection conditions

- source capture is incomplete or unauthorized
- the desired effect or audience remains materially ambiguous
- observations and proposals are presented as the same evidence class
- motion or rendering cost has no narrative justification or fallback
- acceptance relies on one attractive frame
- the selected delegate lacks the required artifact or authority

## termination conditions

- one dominant route owns the current mission
- observed extracted inferred and proposed claims remain distinguishable
- one coherent visual law governs the system
- accessibility performance fallbacks and unknowns are explicit
- the next specialist receives a bounded handoff
- acceptance can fail against stated rejection criteria

## proof limits

- artifact-review-only
- no-self-certification
- no-runtime-observation-without-an-observation-receipt
