## Comparison against the installed owner set

The current release has 68 owner entrypoints. The selected topic cluster intersects most strongly with:

- **Logos:** already binds source, audience, rights, and claim class; authors evidence-derived reports, narratives, and documents; checks support, accessibility, and whether claims exceed sources. It does not spell out a slide-specific production artifact contract with page/claim lineage and output-package/render verification.
- **Muse:** already owns visual direction, motion, accessibility, responsive states, and deterministic visual acceptance. It does not own the factual claim ledger or authored business-deck artifact.
- **Atlas:** already owns metric semantics, analytics, schemas, and measured operational evidence. Candidate deck work must consume approved analysis, not compute or reinterpret business metrics.
- **Agora and Beacon:** already reconcile business evidence and support truthful client, market, offer, and lifecycle decisions. A candidate cross-asset check must not replace those analysis routes or invent investor facts.
- **Venture falsification, cashflow forecasting, and financial statement reconciliation:** own thesis testing and numerical/financial analysis. A polished fundraising pack is not evidence of demand, financial assurance, or investment advice.

This comparison leaves two bounded seams, not a general presentations or investor-advice domain: (R1) the gap between evidence-grounded narrative and format-specific slide-package validation; and (R2) explicit drift detection when the same defined business fact is reused across several investor artifacts. The proposed contracts in candidate-methods.md deliberately route analysis and visual judgment back to existing specialists. If either seam is judged already covered by an uninspected product reference or future owner method, drop it rather than expanding scope.
