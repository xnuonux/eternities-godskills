# Business evidence-room decision packet

Use this conditional business-process-operations extension when one account,
opportunity, or service issue needs a decision-ready internal packet across
health, pipeline, metrics, escalation, and evidence-room records. Route broader
account reconstruction to Agora and broader product-operations routing to
Prometheus. This method does not configure a CRM, assign a universal health
score, contact a customer, change records, make commitments, or provide legal,
financial, investment, or tax advice.

## Method: evidence-room closure ledger

Bind one subject identity, as-of time, decision question, audience, and allowed
sources. Keep candidate or unmatched identities separate. For every
decision-relevant signal, record its locator, observation time, owner, and claim
class: observed, derived, assumed, proposed, unknown, or contradicted. Missing
inputs stay missing; they are not zeros.

Create one ledger spanning these domains:

- account lifecycle, usage, support, stakeholder, value, renewal, or expansion
  signals, without compressing them into a score unless one was supplied;
- pipeline state using verifiable stage entry or exit facts, next step, owner,
  close rationale, and risk, while separating creation, progression, and
  forecast inspection;
- metric definition, period, baseline, segment, transformation, observed
  movement, plausible drivers, and data-quality caveats;
- escalation reason, impact, status, requested decision and owner, evidence,
  prior attempts, and unknowns; and
- an evidence-room matrix listing each required item, purpose, present/missing/
  stale/restricted/unknown state, locator or version, as-of date, reviewer, and
  contradiction or validation note.

The matrix answers whether a document is available. It does not establish the
document's truth, completeness, approval, legal status, financial accuracy, or
customer authorization. Keep supporting and contradicting records visible.

Close the packet with the decision options supported by the record: proceed,
hold, request evidence, or route an internal escalation. For each option show
the evidence, counterevidence, unresolved dependency, owner, and next check;
separate performed local drafting from proposed account, customer, financial,
or external effects. Use supplied policy for urgent security, privacy, billing,
legal, data-loss, outage, or executive-account escalation. Do not infer
severity, probability, renewal outcome, or timing when the evidence or policy
is absent. Finish when the decision owner can act or can name the exact missing
evidence.

This is an analytical and drafting method only. It does not send messages,
update a CRM, approve a renewal, promise a fix, discount, refund, or forecast,
or claim runtime or business-outcome improvement.
