# Conditional reference: evidence-bearing stakeholder updates

Use this reference only when the request is a factual status update for a named audience and the result must coordinate an action, decision, response, or next checkpoint. It is not a generic letter template, a platform sender, investment/legal advice, or a replacement for a Chorus route.

Before drafting, record:

- the speaking identity and relationship, accepted voice evidence, intended recipients, audience need, purpose, and channel role;
- the reporting period, source inventory and freshness, and each claim as observed, supplied, derived, current, forward-looking, recommendation, or unresolved;
- every metric's definition, formula, unit, denominator, scope, source, and time window;
- the requested action or decision, owner, due date, dependency, authority, sensitivity, minimum disclosure, and accessibility or language needs.

Produce two linked local artifacts.

1. **Factual update:** state the period and what changed, what did not, evidence-backed progress or learning, risks/gaps/unknowns, next focus, and any specific ask or decision. Separate actuals from forward-looking statements and label both. Tailor register and explanation to the audience while preserving the supplied voice. For family or learner material, describe supplied observations and a practical support or next step without diagnosis, inferred motivation, or unnecessary identifiers. For investor or stakeholder material, preserve exact metric definitions and windows and flag financial, customer, fundraising, or legal claims for responsible review. For official material, use only verified identity, recipient, jurisdiction, and supplied authority.

2. **Coordination ledger:** for each requested action or decision, record the item, owner, deadline, evidence or authority, dependencies, status, fallback or escalation owner, and disclosure or approval state. Include only what the recipient needs.

Check that every material claim traces to a source and date, qualifiers survive compression, metrics are not renamed or rounded without a note, identity or voice gaps remain visible, and restricted personal or confidential details are minimized or separated. Use readable structure and equivalent access; do not promise a translation or accessibility feature that is not supplied.

Return the update, ledger, review notes, unresolved factual checks, and next owner as local drafts. Keep drafting, review, scheduling, publication, reply, and sending distinct. Record any proposed external effect (target, exact payload, authority, approval, reversibility, and risk), then stop unless a separately authorized route performs it. This reference grants no permission to send email, letters, webhooks, or public posts, and does not certify legal, financial, investment, or learning outcomes.
