# Evidence-bearing stakeholder updates

Use this method when a substantial update must both explain supplied evidence and coordinate a decision, response, or next step. It fits a project briefing, community notice, family update, or investor newsletter; it is unnecessary for a routine isolated sentence. No particular platform, voice-profile file, organization, or delivery service is required.

## Shape the message for its recipient

Identify who is speaking, who will read it, the reporting period, the purpose, and the authorized scope. Reuse context already provided. Apply supplied voice examples without inventing the speaker's experiences, commitments, credentials, or relationship. If no examples exist, choose clear language appropriate to the audience. Preserve warmth and specificity instead of replacing the message with a compliance template.

Select only the facts needed by this audience. Keep internal assessment, private support details, and reviewer-only questions outside the recipient draft. A person-facing concern should describe dated observations and available support, not diagnose, assign character, or turn an uncertain inference into a verdict. Make dates, actions, contact routes, and any access alternatives unambiguous; leave unknown details visibly unresolved in review notes, not fabricated in polished copy.

## Keep claims stable through compression

For material metrics, retain the measure, unit, time window, population or denominator, comparison basis, source, and relevant limitation. Compute only what those inputs support. Distinguish relative change from percentage-point change and separate observed movement from an explanation of why it happened. A changed definition or cohort can make a numerical comparison invalid even when the arithmetic is correct.

Separate completed work, current observations, targets, forecasts, and proposals. Preserve a meaningful conflict or uncertainty instead of smoothing it away to sound confident. Trace consequential claims in lightweight review notes; every sentence does not need a visible evidence label.

## Make the next step usable

End with the action or decision, its owner, timing, dependency, and current approval state where relevant. A short action section is enough for a simple update. Use a separate coordination table only when several owners or dependencies would otherwise be confusing. Do not silently invent an owner, deadline, consent, or commitment.

Review the recipient draft and internal notes separately: factual accuracy, metric comparability, intended voice, unnecessary disclosure, readable structure, and actionable next steps. Confirm that the recipient version cannot accidentally expose reviewer notes. Report whether the result is a draft, reviewed artifact, scheduled item, or actually delivered message, based on evidence.

This method grants no sending or account authority. Continue an already-authorized delivery through the available host tools when the target and required facts are settled; otherwise hand off the usable draft and exact unresolved item. Never claim delivery from drafting alone.
