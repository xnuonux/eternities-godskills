# Eternities Chorus

Chorus is for communication work where identity, audience, channel role, claims, accessibility, and public risk must stay aligned. It can create local drafts and operational artifacts, and it can implement an explicitly authorized local change; publication and sensitive public decisions remain distinct evidence and effects.

## Choose the route

- **Identity and channel strategy** settles who speaks, to whom, why, where, with what voice evidence, capacity, cadence, and anti-goals.
- **Editorial production** turns approved evidence into original posts, stories, captions, scripts, derivatives, or visual briefs with claim provenance, rights, access, and review state.
- **Community operations** builds a charter, listening queue, triage, response draft, escalation record, or moderation-ready policy. Preserve human ownership of sanctions, crises, and sensitive replies.
- **Measurement and stewardship** defines metrics and windows, validates calculations, runs bounded experiments, and records a learning decision without treating attention as truth.
- **Preview validation** checks page metadata and share assets across declared local parsers before a human or publisher acts.

## Working method

1. Bind the speaking identity, audience hypothesis, purpose, channel role, source inventory, voice evidence, capacity, accessibility needs, and acceptance owner.
2. Keep every material claim tagged as observed, supplied, derived, current, heuristic, creative, judged, or unresolved. Preserve caveats and exact numbers through compression.
3. Design equivalent access through readable structure, captions or transcripts, text alternatives, contrast, restrained motion, and nonvisual cues.
4. Separate drafting, review, scheduling, publication, reply, moderation, account access, scraping, enrichment, spending, and generation. An exact draft is not a sent message.
5. For community work, record item, risk, urgency, evidence, proposed response or non-response, policy basis, human owner, and disposition.

## Deliverable and finish

Return one identity contract, content package, community packet, measurement packet, or preview-validation receipt with source links, assumptions, gaps, review state, performed local effects, and proposed effects. Finish when the artifact is ready for its named owner and no public action is implied. Use [methods.md](references/methods.md) for share-preview validation.

When an evidence-bearing stakeholder update must coordinate decisions or next steps, use [stakeholder-updates.md](references/stakeholder-updates.md). Keep recipient copy separate from reviewer notes; a simple message does not need a separate ledger.

An identity, editorial, or measurement packet is a checkpoint when the user also requested an authorized local content or interface change. Continue into that change and verify it without asking again for permission already granted. Pause only for missing authority or material identity, rights, safety, accessibility, or evidence risk.

Example: validate a page's preview metadata, update the authorized local head tags and fallback image text, and rerun the parser matrix before handing off any public publication decision.
