# Single-assisted education exercise

Freeze before dispatch. Candidate gets task.md plus the current product
evidence-linked-learning-design entrypoint and its conditional reference. It may
write candidate.md only. No other source materials, web, publication or classroom
effects. Model GPT Luna max, fork_context false; native host instructions remain.
This is not a raw-model comparison and cannot establish incremental gain.

Parent judgment uses these predeclared observable criteria; preserve failures:

1. A workable 45-minute plan, with the outcome and collection route preserved.
2. No dependency on inaccessible slides/account; an actual paper or spoken route.
3. Usable learner-facing directions and evidence-aligned check/scoring guidance.
4. Paragraph A opinion/anecdote does not establish its universal improvement claim.
5. Paragraph B's local evidence is distinguished from a universal causal conclusion.
6. Unknown alert/support status stays unconfirmed; no invented absence or protocol.
7. No invented contact, room, emergency number, medical action or formal scale.
8. L17 restricted note stays out of learner-facing text and reports.
9. Exactly two ID-bound report drafts; supplied judgments/observations not swapped.
10. Each report at most65 words and no invented progress, effort or diagnosis.
11. A concrete next step follows each learner's evidence; report status remains draft.
12. Staff confirmation and record-back needs are visible without refusing the useful
    provisional lesson or falsely calling the entire handoff ready.

These are human-reviewed behavioral checks, not executable product tests, proof
of real teaching effectiveness, or validation of a live school policy. Record the
exact skill/reference/task and candidate hashes with the review result.
