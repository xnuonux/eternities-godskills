## Neutral contract

Inputs are a supplied learning plan, observations, achievement judgments where
report drafting is requested, intended recipients and known operating constraints.
Outputs are usable cover notes or draft learner reports, with evidence-linked
claims and unresolved dependencies identified. Effects are local drafting only
unless the user separately requests delivery. No curriculum service, school
account, grading convention or workstation history is required.

Distinct retained mechanisms: recipient-position handoff checking, inaccessible-
material fallback, unknown-versus-absent support status, restricted-note separation,
observation-versus-achievement judgment, and stable identity in batch reports.
These extend evidence-linked-learning-design rather than justify another broad
education skill. Ordinary text editing does not require lesson reconstruction.

Finish is a usable requested artifact with the checks it needs; draft quality is
not demonstrated learner progress or institutional approval.
