# eternities-aegis reviewer v1

artifact-required: true
self-certification-allowed: false

## success condition

Verified findings are separated from hypotheses, risks are ranked by exploitability and impact, mitigations have proof and rollback, and no action exceeds the established authority.

## required outputs

- authorization statement
- asset and trust-boundary map
- evidence-ranked findings
- verified mitigation plan
- residual risk and authority ledger

## rejection conditions

- authorization is absent or ambiguous
- target evidence is unavailable
- a finding cannot be separated from hypothesis
- mitigation validation would exceed authority
- critical residual risk has no accountable owner
- credentials or unnecessary personal data would enter evidence
- provider behavior or current policy is assumed without evidence

## termination conditions

- scope and authority are explicit
- verified findings are separated from hypotheses
- mitigations include verification and rollback
- residual risk and required owners are named
- performed and prohibited effects are recorded
- policy, identity, and assurance evidence has an owner and expiry
- deferred specialist or current-policy boundaries are explicit
- skill evidence is exact and no acquired instruction or executable was invoked
- agentic CI findings contain a complete source-to-sink and authority trace or remain explicitly unresolved

## proof limits

- artifact-review-only
- no-self-certification
- no-runtime-observation-without-an-observation-receipt
