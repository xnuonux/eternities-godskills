# Prometheus mining receipt

Prometheus is independently written from the seven candidate clusters in `product-operations-clusters-v1`. Exact cluster and review digests are recorded in the capability contract and synthesis. The deferred leadership, governance, provider-bound, finance/procurement, and false-positive clusters contribute no routes, source ids, or doctrine. No source prose is copied and no external mutation is performed.

The synthesis preserves the candidate mechanisms: narrow route selection, supplied-measure analytics, source-labeled insight, public-evidence qualification, learner progression, operational tradeoffs, delivery handoffs, explicit effect separation, and fail-closed authority/evidence behavior.

Fixtures verify contract shape, exact candidate evidence, all seven routes, deterministic direct/paraphrase/exclusion/conflict outcomes, and promotion gates. They do not prove live-model interpretation, current external facts, outreach, commercial success, private-data acquisition, production delivery, or renderer correctness.
