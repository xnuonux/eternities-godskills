# Chorus operating contract

Use this reference when communication work is consequential, crosses routes, reconstructs identity, relies on current public evidence, changes community policy, evaluates performance, or approaches a public action.

## universal state

Every artifact records schema version, mission identity, owning route, speaking identity, voice evidence, audience hypothesis, purpose, channel role, source inventory, claim ledger, accessibility constraints, capacity, anti-goals, assumptions, unresolved decisions, acceptance checks, performed local effects, proposed external effects, handoffs, and human-sensitive decision status.

Claims are observed source fact, user-confirmed constraint, derived value, current external evidence, heuristic, creative hypothesis, recommendation, human judgment, or unresolved conflict. A later artifact cannot silently replace its inputs.

## data contracts

### identity and voice

Records the real speaker, role, audience relationship, values, recurring language and structures, observed absences, declared boundaries, representative samples, contradictions, confidence, and acceptance owner. A pattern requires repeated evidence. Sparse samples stay sparse.

### audience hypothesis and channel role

Records the people served, evidence for their needs, excluded assumptions, decision stage, accessibility needs, channel job, desired non-coercive next action, content supply, cadence capacity, and testable success condition. Public activity is not contact consent.

### editorial brief and content package

The brief records source claim, proof, audience, purpose, voice, format, promise, payoff, required assets, rights, accessibility, channel constraints, and review criteria. The package records each original derivative, source links, material changes, character or media constraints, review state, and publication status. Draft and publication are distinct.

### claim provenance

Each claim has an id, text, class, source, source date, transformation notes, confidence, freshness need, allowed contexts, prohibited exaggerations, and reviewer. Compression preserves caveats and exact numbers.

### community charter

Records community purpose, participant dignity, welcome and safety norms, prohibited conduct, participation and conflict principles, privacy, accessibility, escalation levels, moderator authority, sanctions requiring human judgment, crisis owner, and appeal path.

### moderation and escalation

Records item id, public source, risk, urgency, affected participants, evidence, proposed response or non-response, policy basis, required human owner, performed effects, and final disposition. Agents may triage and draft locally. Humans own sensitive replies, sanctions, crisis communication, and public commitments.

### external effect ledger

Every proposed scheduling, publication, reply, moderation, account, scraping, enrichment, spend, generation, or external-communication effect records target, exact payload, purpose, authority, secrets, cost, reversibility, risk, duplicate prevention, acceptance evidence, and operator. No ledger row grants authority.

### metric dictionary

Each metric has a stable name, definition, formula, unit, source, scope, time window, denominator, exclusions, freshness, known bias, and decision served. Similar labels do not imply equivalent metrics.

### experiment, finding, and learning cycle

An experiment records hypothesis, mechanism, baseline, variant, audience, channel, primary and guardrail metrics, sample or observation requirement, start and stop conditions, confounds, and authority. A finding records raw evidence, calculation, uncertainty, alternatives, and whether it is descriptive or causal. A learning cycle records the decision, bounded change, owner, next observation, and rollback.

## route outputs

- identity and channel strategy: identity contract, audience hypotheses, channel map, editorial architecture, capacity-aware calendar, profile proposals, decisions, and acceptance owner;
- editorial production: brief, original drafts, derivatives, visual or media briefs, claim ledger, access checks, review state, and publication status;
- community operations: charter, listening or response queue, triage evidence, draft responses, escalation packet, human owner, and no autonomous public action;
- measurement and stewardship: source coverage, metric dictionary, validated calculations, experiments, findings, learning decisions, uncertainty, and proposed changes.

## failure behavior

- unresolved identity, audience, proof, rights, or voice: stop affected production and request the smallest missing evidence;
- stale platform, trend, policy, or benchmark evidence: preserve the plan and request a current source-labeled refresh;
- unsupported claim or compression drift: remove it or restore caveats before acceptance;
- inaccessible format: block acceptance until an equivalent route exists;
- unclear metric, denominator, join, or window: withhold comparison and causal language;
- harassment, discrimination, impersonation, manipulation, or participant harm: refuse and escalate;
- missing account or external authority: preserve the exact draft and effect ledger, then stop;
- crisis, sanction, sensitive reply, or final identity dispute: hand to the named human owner;
- recursive or background continuation: terminate after the current bounded artifact.

## acceptance

One route owns each operation. Identity, audience, channel role, claims, voice, access, and capacity agree. Every derivative traces to approved evidence. Community policy preserves dignity and human-sensitive judgment. Metrics retain definitions and uncertainty. Local and external effects are separate. Termination leaves no recursive Chorus call, implied publication, autonomous account action, or hidden monitoring loop.
