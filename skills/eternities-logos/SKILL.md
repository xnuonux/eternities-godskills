# eternities logos

Logos makes a bounded local writing artifact or structural reading from supplied material. It is agent-neutral: preserve the user's voice and declared canon without pretending to possess identity, authority, current knowledge, or publication access.

## choose one route

- **technical writing**: explain technical subject matter for a named audience with explicit scope, terms, examples, caveats, and review points.
- **concise editorial**: improve clarity, order, tone, and economy while recording material tradeoffs and preserving meaning.
- **documentation production**: plan, draft, update, or review a versioned document with audience, source, maintenance owner, and change boundary visible.
- **release and reporting**: turn verified project facts into status, release, or report artifacts; never imply completion, impact, or freshness that the evidence does not establish.
- **product and internal narrative**: shape product, organizational, or stakeholder material around context, problem, evidence, decision, consequence, and next action without inventing strategy or proof.
- **structural review**: inspect boundaries, concepts, states, invariants, dependencies, and blast radius; report observations separately from recommendations.
- **structural extraction**: read supplied material into a bounded intermediate representation of headings, claims, entities, relations, decisions, gaps, and provenance. Extraction is read-only and is not permission to reproduce protected text.
- **lessons in structure**: make reasoning durable through sequence, contrast, examples, constraints, and decision points embedded in the artifact rather than detached slogans.

## operating loop

1. State the artifact, audience, source boundary, canon boundary, freshness need, and allowed effects.
2. Build a source ledger. Tag each material statement as supplied fact, canon, user-confirmed constraint, derived value, current evidence, heuristic, creative hypothesis, human judgment, or unresolved conflict.
3. Select one route and preserve its distinct output. If several routes are needed, hand off a typed local artifact rather than blending their contracts.
4. Draft from the ledger. Keep voice and uncertainty visible. Mark missing evidence, disagreement, assumptions, and decisions that require a human owner.
5. Review structure: claim support, audience fit, terminology, ordering, accessibility, maintenance or release state, and whether any sentence exceeds its source.
6. Return the artifact, provenance ledger, unresolved questions, exclusions, and proposed next owner. A draft is not a publication.

## fail closed

- Do not fabricate facts, quotations, metrics, citations, sources, dates, completion, authority, customer evidence, or current claims. Unsupported current claims require fresh authoritative evidence and otherwise remain unknown.
- Do not silently mutate canon, flatten a supplied voice into a generic imitation, or copy protected source prose. Paraphrase only when the source and rights permit it; otherwise extract structure or refuse.
- Refuse or defer plagiarism, close imitation of a living creator, rights-sensitive reuse, confidential material without authorization, publication, scheduling, external writes, account actions, purchases, commits, deployment, or other operational effects.
- Treat provider-specific, runtime-specific, visual-generation, slide-rendering, translation, and incomplete templates as outside this portable skill unless a separate verified contract is supplied.
- When sources conflict, preserve both claims, authority, scope, and uncertainty. Never resolve a conflict by averaging or inventing a compromise.

## termination

Finish when one bounded local artifact or structural representation exists, its provenance and uncertainty are visible, voice and canon are preserved, unsupported claims and effects are refused or handed off, and no publication or external action is implied.
