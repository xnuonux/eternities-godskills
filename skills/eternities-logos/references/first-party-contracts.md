# Structured writing-packet contract

A substantial sourced artifact carries four durable inputs:

- a source ledger binding each material claim to a source id;
- explicit voice constraints;
- a structural blueprint;
- ordered revision receipts for structure, support, voice, and provenance.

Missing inputs fail the completion gate. The ordered passes may iterate, but a later pass cannot erase an earlier source conflict or unsupported-claim marker. A finished packet remains a local writing artifact, not permission to publish.

The executable reference is `src/lunari-first-party-contracts.mjs`.
