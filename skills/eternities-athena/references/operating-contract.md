# Eternities Athena operating contract

Use this reference for consequential study appraisal, prospective design, or multi-study synthesis.

## evidence table

For each claim or study record:

- source identity, version, date, and exact locator;
- claim, population, exposure or intervention, comparator, outcome, and time;
- study design, sampling frame, allocation, controls, and follow-up;
- measurement validity and missing-data handling;
- prespecified versus exploratory analyses;
- effect estimate, uncertainty, model, assumptions, and multiplicity control;
- identified biases, confounders, mediators, colliders, and selection paths;
- directness, precision, consistency, replication, and generalizability;
- supported conclusion, unsupported extension, confidence, and decisive unknown.

## design validity

Match the design to the inference:

- descriptive designs estimate observed distributions under their sampling and measurement limits;
- cross-sectional and ordinary observational associations do not alone identify temporal or causal direction;
- longitudinal evidence may establish ordering but still requires a defensible confounding strategy;
- randomized allocation can support causal inference only when allocation, adherence, attrition, measurement, analysis, and interference assumptions remain credible;
- natural experiments and quasi-experiments require explicit identification assumptions and falsification checks;
- prediction quality does not establish mechanism or intervention effect.

## statistical validity

Identify the estimand before judging a test. Examine effect sizes and uncertainty before threshold labels. Require multiplicity handling when several outcomes, subgroups, models, looks, or hypotheses could generate the reported result. Check assumptions, missingness, dependence, model flexibility, calibration, robustness, and whether the sample supports the claimed resolution.

Do not infer:

- practical importance from a small p-value;
- equivalence or no effect from non-significance without an appropriate design and interval;
- replication from repeated analyses of overlapping data;
- causality from adjustment alone;
- generality from one convenient population;
- certainty from a hierarchy label without study-specific appraisal.

## synthesis

Keep studies distinct before combining them. Reconcile whether they address the same construct and estimand, share samples or teams, differ in intervention, comparator, measurement, follow-up, or risk of bias, and show explainable heterogeneity. A pooled result cannot repair systematically biased inputs. Contradictory high-quality evidence remains visible.

## output

Return the bounded question, evidence map, strengths, validity threats by severity, statistical and causal judgment, synthesis judgment when applicable, strongest supported conclusion, unsupported extensions, confidence and unknowns, and evidence or design changes that would alter the verdict.

Formal medical, regulatory, ethical, or domain-certification conclusions require the relevant professional and complete evidence. Athena may organize the evidence but does not impersonate that authority.
