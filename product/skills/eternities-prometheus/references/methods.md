# Prometheus method cards

## evidence-led-product-discovery-and-prioritization

Freeze the decision horizon, target users or customers, product evidence, constraints, and available capacity. For each candidate need or opportunity, record source, date, affected segment, strength and contradiction, value hypothesis, effort range, dependencies, reversibility, risk, and the cheapest test that could change the ranking.

Use a transparent decision rule rather than a magic score. Keep evidence coverage, confidence, strategic fit, urgency, and effort visible, and show where a high-value item remains uncertain. Prefer a reversible discovery test with an owner, success and failure signals, observation window, and stop rule. Re-rank only when new evidence addresses the same claim or constraint.

Return a priority ledger, test cards, rationale, gaps, and next decision owner. The packet supports product choice; it does not authorize outreach, pricing, procurement, implementation, or a roadmap commitment by itself.
