# Financial statement reconciliation

Use this method to turn supplied statements or a forecast into a traceable analytical model. It supports business understanding and review; it does not confer audit assurance or replace qualified financial advice.

## Establish comparable inputs

Identify the entity, consolidation scope, reporting period, currency, units, accounting basis, restatements, and whether each figure is reported, estimated or derived. Retain source page or cell references. Distinguish annual, year-to-date and single-quarter values; do not mix them in ratios. If a current rule or filing is material, verify it against an authoritative current source.

Keep the original figures intact. Store normalizations and adjustments as visible transformations with explanations. Missing information stays missing rather than becoming zero.

## Reconcile before interpreting

1. Check that the balance sheet balances within the stated rounding tolerance. Tie beginning cash, movement and ending cash across periods.
2. Reconcile income to operating cash flow using the supplied adjustments. Separate operating, investing and financing movements; a financing inflow is not evidence of profitable operations.
3. Compare periods on a consistent perimeter and basis. Isolate acquisitions, currency effects, policy changes and one-off items where evidence supports doing so.
4. For each ratio, state numerator, denominator, unit, period and source. Handle a zero or negative denominator explicitly. Choose beginning, ending or average balances deliberately; do not silently substitute whichever number is available.
5. Build a driver bridge for changes that matter to the user's question. Distinguish price, volume, mix, cost and working-capital explanations from untested hypotheses.
6. If forecasting, separate assumptions from formulas. Show base, downside and upside cases plus sensitivity to the largest uncertain drivers. Do not assign scenario probabilities without a basis.

## Review the interpretation

Select materiality and comparison context before flagging anomalies. A universal percentage-change threshold is not a substitute for the entity's scale, seasonality, business model or accounting context. Diverging earnings and cash can justify investigation; it does not establish misconduct.

For liquidity, consider timing, restrictions, commitments and funding access rather than a single ratio. For profitability, distinguish operating economics from financing, tax and exceptional items. For valuation questions, hand over the reconciled inputs and uncertainty rather than turning a spreadsheet into a guaranteed price prediction.

## Output and finish

Deliver a source table, reconciliation exceptions, formula definitions, adjustment log, scenario results, and a concise account of supported conclusions and unanswered questions. Where a workbook is requested, preserve formulas and provide a visible check sheet.

Finish when a reviewer can trace every material number, reproduce the calculations, identify open discrepancies and see which assumptions would change the conclusion. Label an analytical review as an analytical review—not an audit, certification, personalized investment recommendation or promise of return.
