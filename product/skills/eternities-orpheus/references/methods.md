# Orpheus method cards

## Transcription and alignment

Record source identity, file or stream condition, language, sampling or time base, supplied speaker labels, diarization status, timestamp precision, confidence representation, and review owner. Preserve uncertain words, overlaps, omissions, and inaudible spans instead of silently normalizing them. Validate a transcript against the source at representative start, middle, end, overlap, silence, and formatting boundaries.

## Timeline and provenance

Represent each approved asset with source, rights or consent state, duration, offset, format, transformation, and review state. Make the timeline auditable through a beat table and duration check. Verify that captions or transcript handoffs align with the same time base and that a local preview is not mistaken for publication.
