# Agora method cards

## research-proposal-planning-and-review

Treat a proposal as a criterion-bound draft packet. Begin with the active solicitation or decision brief: eligibility, aims, review criteria, budget rules, dates, required sections, and evidence standard. If the governing call is missing or stale, mark submission fit unresolved.

Build an aims-to-evidence matrix. For every aim, state the question, supported observation, proposed method, measurable output, dependency, risk, and the result that would weaken the aim. Separate literature or supplied evidence from a design choice, estimate, and promised outcome. Make the budget a transparent assumption table with units, dependencies, eligible and ineligible items, and unresolved approvals; do not invent rates or imply funding success.

Assemble the packet in the reader's order: problem and significance, aims, approach, feasibility, evaluation, timeline, resources, risks, and requested decisions. Run a criterion-by-criterion review that records pass, partial, missing, or contradicted evidence, then append a revision ledger with owner and next check. The deliverable is submission-ready as a local draft only when the solicitation, sources, gaps, and human approvals are visible.

## Score and identity checks

Keep unmatched records and partial dimensions in the account packet. If a score is needed, show the original and effective denominator and why any adjustment occurred. For identity reconciliation, retain original values and merge only after independent stable evidence agrees; otherwise return candidate identities and the smallest disambiguating fact.
