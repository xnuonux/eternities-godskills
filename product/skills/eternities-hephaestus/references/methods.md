# Hephaestus worksheet

Use this compact record for each candidate:

- workload, input class, output bound, quality floor, and hard constraints;
- model revision, precision, runtime, hardware, batch, concurrency, context, sampling, dataset, metric, and measurement date;
- measured facts, official specifications, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns;
- weight, cache, activation, runtime, workspace, fragmentation, and safety-headroom estimates;
- compatibility, privacy, residency, license, provenance, observability, rollback, and cost gates;
- matched benchmark, repetitions, percentiles, failure threshold, and decision.

Reject a candidate when an unknown hard constraint remains. A capacity formula can guide a benchmark but cannot certify fit, quality, speed, or operational cost.
