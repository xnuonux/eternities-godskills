# Measured paid creative iteration

Use this entrypoint to turn observed campaign evidence into a small creative decision. It can support actual authorized production and launch work, but it never treats the skill as permission to spend, target people, or change an ad account.

## Separate observation from hypothesis

Start with the evidence window, platform, placement, creative identifiers, spend or exposure denominator, attribution definition, audience aggregation level, and known data gaps. Label each statement as observed, derived, hypothesis, or unknown. Do not infer sensitive traits, intent, or causality from a segment label that does not support it.

Write one hypothesis in a falsifiable form: a specific creative change, for a stated offer and context, is expected to change a primary metric within a declared window while staying inside guardrails. Use supplied brand truth and claims constraints. If the evidence is not comparable by placement, audience, attribution, or time, say so before designing variants.

## Design the iteration

1. Create the smallest useful contrast. Change one causal feature where possible—opening frame, benefit order, proof treatment, call to action, format, or copy angle—while holding other material variables constant.
2. Define primary, secondary, and guardrail metrics with denominators, attribution windows, minimum observation period, and a decision threshold. Include quality or complaint signals where conversion alone could reward a harmful outcome.
3. Set the budget ceiling, launch owner, audience scope, test duration, stopping rules, and treatment of early or incomplete data. A precommitted rule should say what happens for win, null, loss, underpowered, and instrumentation-failure outcomes.
4. Prepare the creative variants, naming scheme, evidence links, measurement template, and approval handoff. If the user has authorized the actual experiment, execute only the declared variants and window; otherwise leave the packet ready for that decision.
5. Read results at the end of the declared window. Do not declare a winner because of a first-day fluctuation, an unbalanced split, or a metric that was changed after launch.

## Finish and limits

The output is a creative hypothesis packet, variant ledger, metric definitions, guardrails, launch or no-launch decision, and residual uncertainty. Record any synthetic preview or authored fixture as design material, never as campaign performance. Finish when the evidence, contrast, resource limit, measurement plan, and human control point are explicit.

## Common failure modes

- Turning a correlation into an audience claim.
- Changing creative, audience, placement, and attribution at once.
- Optimizing a proxy while ignoring complaints, retention, or cost guardrails.
- Moving the measurement window after seeing an unfavorable result.
- Calling a prepared draft or mock launch an actual paid action.
