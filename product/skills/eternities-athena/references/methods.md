# Athena method cards

## Evidence-table starter

For every material claim or study, record identity, version, date, locator, population, exposure or intervention, comparator, outcome, time, design, sampling, allocation, measurement, missingness, prespecified analysis, effect and uncertainty, assumptions, bias, confounding, directness, precision, consistency, and the strongest conclusion supported. Keep unsupported extensions and decisive unknowns beside the conclusion.

## Falsification and calibration

Write the observation that would reject each live explanation before interpreting results. For mechanism or internal-state claims, require an intervention, matched control, independently observed criterion, and prespecified error accounting. A surviving hypothesis is supported only within the tested scope; self-report, a proxy, or a fixture alone does not establish the claim.
