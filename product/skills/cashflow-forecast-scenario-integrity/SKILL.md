# Cash-flow forecast and scenario integrity

Use when a budget, working-capital change or delayed payment must be translated into dated cash consequences. This is an analytical method, not payment authority, investment advice, audit assurance or a forecast-accuracy guarantee. A simple timing question need not become a full financial model.

## Define the cash being forecast

Declare the entity or consolidation perimeter, currency, units, accounting basis, opening balances, actual/forecast cutoff, horizon and bucket boundaries. Distinguish cash balances from cash available for the decision: restrictions, minimum reserves and undrawn financing are not interchangeable. Do not invent available credit or a minimum-cash policy. Choose a resolution that exposes relevant shortfalls; positive month-end cash does not rule out an earlier gap.

Retain source references and actual, estimate or assumption status. Missing values remain unresolved, not zero. Keep currencies separate unless a dated conversion rule is supplied. Separate revaluation and scope adjustments from receipts and payments when reconciling balances.

## Build the settlement ledger

Give each material event a stable identity, amount, currency, recognition period, settlement date or rule, evidence status and source. Distinguish cash movements from accrual/non-cash entries and opening balances. Link an invoice to its collection or payment; do not count both as cash. Track partial settlements, outstanding amounts and events beyond the horizon instead of dropping them.

Apply operating/investing/financing classes where relevant to the declared reporting basis; unresolved classifications stay visible. At a consolidated perimeter, internal transfers must not create external inflows. Preserve account-level movements when they matter to availability.

Working-capital day ratios are timing indicators, not cash amounts or invoice schedules. If used, declare numerator, denominator, period, balance convention and day basis; address zero or negative denominators. Translate any driver change through a stated flow and settlement rule. Do not replace known invoice dates with an unexplained average.

For each bucket, reconcile:

`closing cash = opening cash + receipts - payments + separately identified reconciliation adjustments`

Carry closing cash into the next opening balance. Cash movements tie to settlement events; non-cash entries do not silently become payments. An unexplained difference is an exception, not a balancing plug.

## Isolate scenarios

Start every named alternative from one unchanged base. Record each changed driver, old/new value, rationale, affected event and affected bucket. Recompute dependent values; distinguish direct changes from their consequences. A delayed collection moves cash timing without automatically changing recognized revenue or total collectibility. A haircut is a separate assumption.

Explain both period cash-movement deltas and cumulative closing-balance deltas; they are different quantities. Check every numerical narrative claim against the corresponding table. Keep out-of-horizon settlements and outstanding balances visible when a timing shift changes the horizon total. Check that one-off items appear once, unchanged inputs stay unchanged and unrelated assumptions did not drift. Do not assign scenario probabilities without evidence.

## Check and deliver

Verify opening-to-closing arithmetic, event-to-bucket totals, continuity, duplicate exclusions, signs, currency/basis consistency and scenario attribution. Where headroom is requested, name its availability definition and threshold, then show the size and duration of each gap. Negative projected cash can be a useful warning; it is not automatically a calculation error. State what coarse buckets may conceal.

Return the smallest useful cash ladder, event and scenario-delta ledgers, assumptions, reconciliation exceptions and decision-relevant uncertainty. With unknown settlement dates, provide a labeled sensitivity or partial forecast instead of invented certainty. Finish when a reviewer can reproduce material numbers and see which dated assumptions change the conclusion. This does not establish that funding will be available or the forecast will occur.
