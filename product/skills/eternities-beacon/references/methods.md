# Beacon method cards

## subscription-retention-and-payment-recovery

Begin with aggregate lifecycle evidence: cohort window, plan or segment scope, renewal and failure definitions, payment-state categories, contact basis, and known missingness. Separate involuntary payment failure, voluntary cancellation, dissatisfaction, inactivity, and data-quality artifacts; do not infer a reason from a single event. Preserve the denominator and the cost or harm boundary.

Design interventions that restore informed choice: clear retry or update instructions, accessible reminders where contact is authorized, pause or downgrade options, transparent recovery timing, and a non-contact path. Do not use hidden fees, false urgency, manipulative cancellation friction, or personalized sensitive inference. For each intervention, define owner, eligibility, message or page change, primary metric, guardrails, stop rule, rollback, and review state.

The output is a retention and recovery plan plus a measurement and authorization gate. It may include local copy, configuration, or experiment artifacts when authorized, but it does not imply account mutation, payment capture, outreach, or production deployment. Report what aggregate evidence cannot establish about an individual customer.
