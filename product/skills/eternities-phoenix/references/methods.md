# Phoenix method cards

## Durable elimination ledger

Keep three partitions: verified foundations, eliminated hypotheses with their disconfirming locator, and exactly one current hypothesis with one predeclared check. Each check records expected observation, falsifier, evidence destination, and rollback. A new observation that conflicts with a foundation reopens it explicitly; it does not erase the old evidence.

## Performance and recovery packet

Bind workload, baseline, units, environment, impact, acceptance threshold, proposed intervention, preconditions, rollback, owner, and stop rule. Capture repeated measurements and functional output together. A recovery plan is not a repair receipt; a local repair is not a production recovery unless the target and effect are directly evidenced.
